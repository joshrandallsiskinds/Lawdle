About CanLIIDLE

    

      
CanLIIDLE is a daily Canadian case law deduction game.

      
Use legal clues to identify landmark Canadian appellate decisions.

      
Play: https://joshrandall.github.io/canliidle/

      
After each guess, compare its court, province, area of law, legal issue, outcome, and year with the answer. Exact matches and related legal categories help narrow the field; a new clue appears with each guess.

      
The puzzle is selected from the calendar date and is the same for everyone playing that date. Your progress, theme, and completed-game statistics are stored in this browser.

      
This game is for educational purposes only and does not constitute legal advice. It is independent of CanLII and the courts. Case names and citations identify decisions; summaries and clues are original educational descriptions, not reproduced headnotes or decision text. Judicial reasons and database content may carry copyright or licence terms. Verify citations and applicable CanLII linking terms before public release, and consult the linked reasons for authoritative text.

      
Feedback

      
CanLIIDLE is an independent educational project and is not affiliated with CanLII or the courts. Case summaries are provided for general learning and are not legal advice. Consult the linked decision for authoritative reasons.

    

  

  
    
Privacy

    

      
CanLIIDLE does not ask for or collect personal information. There are no analytics, tracking cookies, advertising identifiers, telemetry, or external assets.

      
Stored on this device

      
Only anonymous gameplay data is stored locally: today's case guesses, aggregate games played and won, streaks, guess distribution, selected category and difficulty, and your theme preference.

      
Why and how

      
This data keeps your daily game, preferences, and statistics available in this browser. It is stored in versioned localStorage keys under CanLIIDLE_v1; it is not sent to a CanLIIDLE server. Clearing this site's browser storage removes it. Private-browsing modes may discard it when the session ends.

      
Opening a case link navigates to CanLII, which is a separate service with its own privacy practices. No case link is loaded automatically.

    

  

  
    
Security & release review

    

      
Security report

      
  • User case guesses are length-limited, normalized, and rendered as text.
  • Dynamic case, clue, result, and statistics views use DOM APIs; external case links are limited to HTTPS CanLII URLs.
  • Storage is treated as untrusted, validated, versioned, and reset to safe defaults when corrupt or unavailable.
  • No query parameters, network requests, analytics, cookies, or third-party scripts are used.

      
Recommended deployment CSP response header:

      
default-src 'none'; script-src 'self' 'sha256-<script-hash>'; style-src 'self' 'sha256-<style-hash>'; img-src 'self' data:; font-src 'self'; connect-src 'none'; base-uri 'none'; form-action 'none'; frame-ancestors 'none'; object-src 'none'; upgrade-insecure-requests

      
Generate hashes for the exact inline script and style at release time, or move them to same-origin files before using a strict policy. This build avoids dynamic style attributes. Send CSP as an HTTP response header; frame-ancestors is not effective in a meta policy. Begin with Content-Security-Policy-Report-Only, then enforce after reviewing reports.

      
Privacy report

      
No personal information or telemetry is collected. Anonymous gameplay, preferences, and local progress stay in browser storage. Clicking CanLII links leaves the app and is subject to CanLII's policies.

      
Legal technology risk

      
Case names and citations are factual identifiers, but judicial reasons and third-party database content may have copyright or licence conditions. The game uses original summaries and links to decisions; it does not reproduce CanLII headnotes or decision text. CanLIIDLE is not affiliated with CanLII, LexisNexis, Thomson Reuters, or any court. Confirm every case record and link before publication.

      
Accessibility report

      
  • Landmarks, heading hierarchy, skip link, labeled controls, live status, visible focus, and keyboard autocomplete are present.
  • Feedback includes icon, text, and color; reduced-motion preferences and safe-area mobile controls are supported.
  • Selected foreground/background pairs were contrast-checked in code. A complete WCAG 2.2 AA conformance claim still requires browser, zoom/reflow, keyboard, and assistive-technology testing.

      
Development diagnostics: on localhost or a local file only, press Ctrl+Shift+D to inspect the current puzzle, metadata, game state, statistics, and versioned local storage. The shortcut is disabled on public hosts.

      
Deployment checklist

      
  • Serve over HTTPS with the CSP and security headers above; add Referrer-Policy: strict-origin-when-cross-origin, X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff, and Permissions-Policy: camera=(), microphone=(), geolocation=().
  • Verify case citations, summaries, and CanLII destinations against authoritative decisions before publication; follow CanLII terms and link guidance.
  • Run Lighthouse and manual keyboard/screen-reader tests at 320px, 375px, 390px, 414px, 768px, and desktop widths.
  • For future PWA support, add a same-origin web manifest, install icons, HTTPS hosting, then a separately reviewed service worker with versioned offline-cache invalidation. Do not cache private browser state or intercept CanLII navigation.
